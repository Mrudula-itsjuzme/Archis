# Architect Interview Guide

Use this after watching the architect work. Do not begin by pitching Archis for ten minutes and then ask whether they like it.

## Before showing Archis

1. Walk me through the last early-stage design revision that became annoying or unexpectedly expensive in time.
2. What triggered the revision?
3. Which parts of the design were easy to change?
4. Which relationships did you actively try to preserve?
5. How did you know a revision had technically worked but made the design worse?
6. Where did you have to repeat work across tools or representations?
7. What do you currently write down as a constraint versus simply keep in your head?

## Intent-marking task

On one real early plan, mark:

- must not change
- strongly preserve
- nice to preserve
- incidental / free to change

Ask the architect to explain each mark aloud. Do not suggest courtyard/privacy/daylight categories unless they bring them up first.

## After showing Archis hypotheses

1. Which hypotheses were useful?
2. Which were obvious noise?
3. What important idea did Archis miss?
4. Was correcting the system faster than explaining the design from scratch?
5. Would you want these hypotheses stored for the project, or are they too changeable?

## After the revision demo

1. Which option would you continue from, if any?
2. What did the scoring get wrong?
3. Was the consequence explanation useful or merely something you could already see?
4. What would make you distrust this system immediately?
5. Would this belong in a separate early-design workspace, inside your existing tool, or nowhere?

## Evidence discipline

Record behavior and concrete examples separately from compliments. `Cool idea` is not validation. A repeated workflow pain, a correction pattern, a chosen candidate, or willingness to use it on another live revision is much stronger evidence.
