Footer: `No moat yet. The moat has to be earned from real architect-confirmed revisions.`

This line is useful because it shows you know the difference between a thesis and a company.

Mention the founder-fit lesson briefly: wanting to build together was not enough; ownership, decision-making and tolerance for experimentation matter.

Close:

**The architect gives Archis the idea. I'm testing whether software can get very good at knowing what not to destroy.**
